Hospitality Sector Case Study

15X Booking Velocity for Aman Serene Resorts

8 Month Campaign
Global Target
Google & Meta Search

The Challenge

Aman Serene Resorts, a high-end collection of hotels, spa sanctuaries, and villas, relied heavily on Online Travel Agents (OTAs) like Expedia and Booking.com for over 78% of their bookings. This dynamic cost them up to 20% in commissions per stay and prevented them from building direct customer relationships. They needed a technical marketing framework to scale high-ticket direct bookings on their own website.

The Strategy

We built a multi-stage direct booking customer journey targeting luxury travelers. The strategy combined high-intent Search keywords (Google Ads), behavioral lookalike campaigns (Meta Ads), and specialized Landing Page optimizations (CRO) to maximize page value.

"By targeting search intent around 'luxury private spa villas' and restructuring booking templates, ADMAZIC helped us bypass OTA commissions completely, scaling our direct revenue by 15x."
— Director of Revenue, Aman Serene Resorts

The Execution Timeline

Month 1-2: Audit & Funnel Setup

Removed conversion bottlenecks from the booking engine. Built custom landing templates highlighting spa and room experiences. Set up clean offline server tracking to attribute bookings correctly.

Month 3-5: Intent Advertising Scaling

Launched targeted Google Search campaigns and Meta custom audience templates. Structured bids to maximize luxury traveler bookings over general traffic.

Month 6-8: CRO Testing & Retention

A/B tested mobile checkout flows, resulting in a 42% lift in conversion rates. Deployed email and retargeting automation for cart drop-offs.

The Final Outcome

Over the 8-month period, Aman Serene bypassed third-party OTAs for direct website stays, reducing average customer acquisition costs by 45%. Website revenue grew from $12,000/month to over $185,000/month, delivering a true 15x direct sales return.
