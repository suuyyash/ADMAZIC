Refund Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026. This policy outlines how ADMAZIC processes and safeguards information across our systems.

Last updated: July 2026

1. No Refunds Policy

At ADMAZIC, we deliver custom growth marketing services, performance advertising campaigns, and custom CRM / web engineering. Because our services require significant developer hours, ad strategy creation, and dedicated account engineering resources, we operate under a strict no-refund policy. All setup fees, project milestones, and retainer payments are final.

2. Service Retainers

Monthly service retainers cover ongoing optimization, reporting, and campaign management. Once a billing cycle begins, the retainer for that period is non-refundable, as scheduling and workload allocations have already been secured for your account.

3. Advertising Spend

Any budget allocated for third-party advertising platforms (e.g. Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads) is paid directly to those platforms. ADMAZIC is not responsible for, and cannot issue refunds on, any media budget spent on external platforms.

4. SLA Cancellation

Clients may request termination of ongoing retainers by providing a 30-day written notice in accordance with their signed SLA. No partial or prorated refunds will be issued for any unused portion of the current billing cycle.
