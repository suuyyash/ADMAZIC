Why Your Current Ads Are Failing

Common pitfalls that drain budgets in high-competition markets.

Broad Keyword Bloodbath

In a city with CPCs as high as Mumbai's, bidding on broad match keywords without a massive negative keyword list is financial suicide. You end up paying exorbitant amounts for informational searches, unqualified clicks, and irrelevant traffic that will never convert into actual revenue.

Optimizing for Clicks, Not Sales

Many agencies report on vanity metrics like impressions and clicks, ignoring the only metric that matters: bottom-line revenue. If your Google Ads setup is not integrated with your CRM to track offline conversions and closed sales, the algorithm is optimizing blindly, resulting in poor lead quality.

Landing Page Disconnect

Sending traffic from a highly specific search ad to a generic, slow-loading homepage destroys conversion rates. In a fast-paced market, if your landing page doesn't immediately solve the user's query and provide a seamless conversion path, they will instantly bounce to a competitor.

Failing to Guide the AI

Modern campaigns like Performance Max rely heavily on AI. However, AI is only as good as the data it receives. Poorly structured conversion tracking, low-quality creative assets, and a lack of audience signals mean the AI spends your budget inefficiently across the wrong networks.
