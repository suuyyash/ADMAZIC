D2C Apparel Sector Case Study

Scaling D2C E-commerce Sales 12X for Aurum & Co.

6 Month Timeline
US & UK Markets
Meta Ads & Shopify CRO

The Challenge

Aurum & Co. is a premium minimalist apparel brand selling eco-conscious wardrobe essentials. While they had a strong brand identity, their website suffered from slow page loading times (LCP of 4.8s) and high cart abandonment rates (74%). Additionally, their Meta advertising campaigns were stuck at a plateau, averaging a break-even 1.2x ROAS due to fatigued creative testing systems.

The Strategy

We launched a double-pronged growth push: rebuilding their Shopify site code for hyper-speed while implementing our proprietary "Dynamic Creative Testing" framework on Meta. This aligned paid ads directly with high-performance landing page variations.

"ADMAZIC rebuilt our product page templates to load instantly on mobile, and completely transformed our Meta ad account. Our ROAS quadrupled in under four months."
— Head of Digital, Aurum & Co.

The Execution Timeline

Month 1: Shopify Code Restructure

Cleaned custom stylesheets, removed three broken apps, and optimized high-resolution model imagery. Reduced mobile LCP to 1.8s, resulting in an immediate 28% increase in store conversion rates.

Month 2-4: Meta Scaling Engine

Implemented target lookalike models and scaled structured creative tests (UGC, styling guides, product features). Increased monthly ad spend while scaling ROAS to 4.2x.

Month 5-6: Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Optimization

Integrated post-purchase upsells and email workflows to capture secondary transactions, driving up Average Order Value (AOV) by 18%.

The Final Outcome

Within 6 months, Aurum & Co. scaled from 800 orders/month to over 9,600 orders/month. Direct Shopify sales rose from $48,000/month to over $580,000/month, maintaining a blended 4.2x ROAS and reducing customer acquisition costs by 38%.
