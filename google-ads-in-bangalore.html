Common Pitfalls Damaging Your Ad ROI

Identifying the leaks that are draining your marketing budget.

Broad Match Chaos

Many agencies rely heavily on broad match keywords, causing your ads to appear for irrelevant, low-intent searches. We implement strict match type controls and exhaustive negative keyword lists to ensure your budget is only spent on users actively looking to buy.

Landing Page Friction

Generating a click is only half the battle. If you send paid traffic to a slow-loading, generic homepage, users will bounce. We design conversion-optimized, fast-loading dedicated landing pages that perfectly align with the ad's promise.

Inadequate Tracking

Without flawless tracking, optimization is just guessing. We see many accounts failing to track phone calls, form fills, or offline sales. We implement advanced GTM setups so Google's AI learns exactly which clicks translate into real revenue.

Ignoring Customer LTV

Focusing solely on the cheapest Cost Per Click (CPC) is a trap. A higher CPC for a highly targeted keyword often yields a vastly superior Customer Lifetime Value (LTV). We optimize bidding strategies based on long-term profitability, not just superficial metrics.
