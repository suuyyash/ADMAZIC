Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026. This policy outlines how ADMAZIC processes and safeguards information across our systems.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you request a Growth Audit, apply for a career role, or contact us. This includes your name, company name, business email, website URL, social profile links, and any uploaded files or project briefs. We also collect anonymous server diagnostics (IP addresses, browser parameters) to maintain security.

2. How We Process Data

Your business and contact metrics are processed exclusively to compile your Growth Audit roadmap, respond to job inquiries, or coordinate strategic review sessions. We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact or business parameters to third parties.

3. Cookies & Server Tagging

Our website utilizes cookies and server-side tracking (Google Tag Manager Server) to analyze conversion paths and optimize page speed. These settings record traffic patterns anonymously to help us build a better user layout. You can disable cookies directly in your browser preferences.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under GDPR and CCPA guidelines, you have the right to request a copy of your personal data logged on our systems, request corrections, or request complete removal. Contact grow@admazic.com to submit a data removal directive.
