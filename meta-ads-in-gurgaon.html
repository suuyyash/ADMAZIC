Common Meta Ads Challenges in Gurgaon

And how our structured approach solves them.

1. Extremely High Competition

The sheer volume of brands fighting for the same affluent audience drives up ad costs significantly. Without a scroll-stopping creative strategy, your brand easily gets lost in the noise.

2. Low Lead Quality (Junk Leads)

A common issue for real estate and B2B services. Broad targeting often results in high volume but poor intent. We solve this by adding friction to forms and implementing robust CRM pre-qualification.

3. iOS 14 Tracking Failures

Apple's privacy updates decimated traditional pixel tracking. Without proper server-side tracking (Conversions API), you are losing critical data, making optimization impossible.

4. Creative Fatigue

Running the same graphic for months leads to ad fatigue, drastically increasing Cost Per Acquisition (CPA). A rapid, continuous pipeline of fresh video content is mandatory.
