Commercial Intent Keyword Mapping

Ranking for a keyword with 10,000 searches a month is completely useless if the searcher is just looking for a free definition. We don't chase vanity volume. We hunt for high-intent, long-tail commercial keywords where the user has their credit card out and is actively looking to hire or buy. Technical SEO: Repairing the Engine

Google can't rank what it can't crawl.

Crawlability & Indexing

We optimize your `robots.txt` and XML Sitemaps, ensuring Googlebot efficiently crawls your most profitable pages without wasting crawl budget on junk URLs.

Core Web Vitals

Site speed is a direct ranking factor. We compress assets, defer scripts, and optimize server response times to hit perfect LCP, FID, and CLS scores.

Architecture & 404s

We fix broken links (404s), implement precise 301 redirects to preserve link equity, and restructure your internal linking to silo authority perfectly.

Heading Hierarchy & Meta Tags

A messy HTML structure confuses search engines. We strictly enforce H1-H6 heading hierarchy, optimize title tags for Click-Through Rate (CTR), and craft meta descriptions that force searchers to click your link over the competition.

Eradicating Duplicate Content

Duplicate content triggers algorithmic suppression. We deploy canonical tags (`rel="canonical"`) and dynamic parameter handling to ensure Google always knows which specific page to index and rank, consolidating your authority.
