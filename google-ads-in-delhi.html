Why Most Google Ads Campaigns Fail in Delhi

Identify the critical errors draining your marketing budget.

Broad Keyword Bidding

Many agencies use broad match keywords without extensive negative keyword lists. In a high-volume market like Delhi, this results in your ads showing for irrelevant, non-commercial searches, instantly draining your budget on clicks that will never convert into actual revenue.

Poor Landing Page Experience

Directing expensive, highly targeted ad traffic to a slow, generic homepage is the most common reason for failure. If the landing page does not precisely match the user's search intent or lacks clear trust signals and a strong call-to-action, the user will bounce immediately, wasting the click cost.

Broken Conversion Tracking

Running Google Ads without meticulous conversion tracking via Google Tag Manager is flying blind. If the Google algorithms cannot accurately measure which keywords and ads are generating form fills or phone calls, they cannot optimize your campaigns, leading to an ever-increasing cost per lead.

Ignoring Ad Copy Testing

Settling for generic ad copy guarantees a low Click-Through Rate (CTR). A low CTR negatively impacts your Quality Score, which subsequently forces you to pay significantly more per click than your competitors for the exact same ad position. Continuous A/B testing of ad creatives is mandatory.
