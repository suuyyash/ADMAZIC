High Ticket Premium Packages for B2B SaaS

Neomaxer, a tech company from Bangalore, came to us with a desire to spend heavily on ads. We insisted on a different approach: fixing the funnel first to avoid burning cash. We offered consultation, optimized their website for CRO, and targeted high-ticket clients for their premium experience packages.

The Leaking Funnel Snapshot

We identified 4 major areas draining their organic potential:

❌ Broken Funnel Their existing funnel was leaking potential high-ticket clients before they could even book a call.
❌ Unoptimized Ad Spend They wanted to spend heavily on ads without a proven conversion mechanism in place.
❌ Poor CRO The website was not optimized to convert premium, high-intent traffic.
❌ Audience Targeting They needed to precisely target decision-makers for premium packages on Meta and Google.

How We Fixed It: The 3-Step Strategy

1 Strategic Consultation

We advised them against burning cash on ads immediately and focused on building a solid foundation first.

2 Funnel & CRO Optimization

We completely revamped their website for Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) and fixed their sales funnel to capture high-ticket clients.

3 Precision Ad Campaigns

Once the funnel was ready, we guided their spend correctly on Meta and Google Ads, targeting the right decision-makers.

The Outcome

In just the first 2 months, our optimized funnel and precision ad campaigns achieved a target of 600 highly qualified leads for their premium tech packages.
