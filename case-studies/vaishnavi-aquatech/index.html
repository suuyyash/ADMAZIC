Taking a Pioneer Business Online

Vaishnavi Aquatech was a pioneer business with a unique Business Model Canvas (BMC) and a very specific target audience in the fisheries sector. They had zero online presence but a strong vision to digitize their operations.

The Leaking Funnel Snapshot

We identified 4 major areas draining their organic potential:

❌ No Online Presence Despite a strong vision, they had completely zero digital footprint to reach new customers.
❌ Niche Target Audience Finding specific buyers in the fisheries sector online required precise audience identification.
❌ Complex Setup They needed a complete digital ecosystem built entirely from scratch.
❌ Unproven Channels It was unclear which platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube) would yield the best results for their niche.

How We Fixed It: The 3-Step Strategy

1 Digital Setup From Scratch

We helped them set up their entire digital presence and accounts from the ground up.

2 Audience Identification

We identified and mapped their highly specific customer segment across platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and YouTube.

3 Pre-Booking Strategy

We launched targeted campaigns to generate demand and secure advance orders for their seeds.

The Outcome

We successfully helped them pre-book their seeds in the first few months, achieving results well over what the client had initially expected.
