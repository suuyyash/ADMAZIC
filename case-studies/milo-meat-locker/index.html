Fixing an Inefficient $700/mo Ad Spend

Milo Meat Locker (based in IA, USA) was spending $700 per month on ads but generating 0 orders for their high-ticket items ($300+ AOV). They needed a complete audit and overhaul of their tracking and checkout experience.

The Leaking Funnel Snapshot

We identified 4 major areas draining their organic potential:

❌ Wasted Ad Spend Spending $700/mo on ads with absolutely zero return on investment.
❌ Broken Tracking Analytics and conversion tracking were not set up properly, blinding ad platforms.
❌ Poor Checkout Flow Friction in the checkout process was causing high cart abandonment rates.
❌ Lack of Trust The website lacked the necessary trust signals to convert high-ticket ($300+) buyers.

How We Fixed It: The 3-Step Strategy

1 Comprehensive Audit

We audited their Meta Ads, Google Ads, and Shopify store to identify the exact points of failure.

2 Tracking & Checkout Overhaul

We fixed their broken tracking setups and streamlined the checkout process to remove friction.

3 CRO & Trust Building

We implemented Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) strategies on the website to build trust for their high-ticket products.

The Outcome

By fixing the tracking and optimizing the checkout flow, we turned their wasted ad spend into 10 high-ticket sales in just the first month.
